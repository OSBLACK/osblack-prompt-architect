# Support

Check [docs/START_HERE.md](docs/START_HERE.md) and existing issues first. For a bug, include the app version, device/OS, expected result, actual result, and safe reproduction steps. Remove personal prompts and credentials from screenshots or logs.

Community support is best-effort. The project cannot provide support for third-party AI accounts, provider billing, modified APKs, unofficial signing services, or devices with unsupported firmware.
